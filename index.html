TIPE · PREPARATORY CLASSES

Ambient Temperature Monitoring and Control Using Arduino & PID

For my TIPE, I developed an Arduino-based temperature monitoring and control system using a PID controller. It brought together programming, electronics, Tinkercad simulations, hands-on experimentation, and thermal modeling.

The project gave me a practical way to explore how software and hardware work together in an automated control system.

Interviews & reflections

THREE PROFESSIONAL PERSPECTIVES

INTERVIEW 01

Damien Wyart

Company
Dataiku — SRE / DevOps Engineer
Career path
Damien Wyart completed scientific preparatory classes before studying engineering at EFREI. His profile lists 23 years of professional experience. He currently works at Dataiku in Paris, France, as an SRE/DevOps engineer specialising in IT infrastructure. His role involves operating and developing a large infrastructure, mainly based in the cloud. His daily responsibilities include technical work on the software and computers that support this infrastructure. He also mentors young people and helps them understand different careers in computing.
Met through
My Job Glasses.
What I learned from him
My conversation with Damien Wyart helped me understand how to prepare for a career in IT. He encouraged me to develop my soft skills alongside my technical abilities and highlighted the importance of learning Linux, particularly for working with systems and infrastructure. He also described DevOps as a promising career path that, in his view, is relatively resilient to AI-driven automation. Finally, he explained that studies provide a foundation, but cover only a small part of the practical knowledge used in everyday work. Many job-specific skills are acquired through experience after graduation, which showed me the importance of remaining curious, adaptable and willing to keep learning throughout my career.

INTERVIEW 02

Ahamada Aboubakar

Company
Capgemini — QA Engineer
Career path
Ahamada Aboubakar earned a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics and Computer Science, followed by a master’s degree in Computer Engineering. After completing internships, he joined an IT services company in Casablanca, Morocco, before moving to France and joining Capgemini as a Quality Assurance (QA) engineer. His role involves ensuring that applications work correctly and reliably before they reach users. He works with project teams to understand requirements and define how software quality will be checked. His daily responsibilities include testing new features, writing test scenarios, identifying bugs and collaborating with developers to resolve them. He also automates tests using code to save time and make quality checks more reliable.
Met through
My Job Glasses.
What I learned from him
My conversation with Ahamada Aboubakar highlighted the importance of teamwork in IT. He showed me how collaboration, clear communication and sharing knowledge contribute to successful projects. He also explained that strong foundations can help us change career direction, even when a particular field becomes saturated. This encouraged me to focus on building transferable skills and remaining open to different opportunities. Regarding my interest in data, he emphasised the importance of mastering mathematics, particularly linear algebra, probability and optimisation. His advice helped me understand how these mathematical foundations can support my ambition to become an expert in data.

INTERVIEW 03

Anahid Hanimyan

Company
EVOSANCE — Founder and IT Recruitment Consultant.
Career path
Anahid Hanimyan trained as an engineer and spent more than 20 years in telecommunications, notably at Bouygues Telecom and Nokia. She worked internationally with technical and innovation teams on technological transformation projects. She then founded EVOSANCE, a recruitment consultancy specialising in IT, data, cloud, cybersecurity and telecommunications. She now helps large companies recruit technology executives and specialists. Her experience gives her an understanding of both technical careers and the skills employers are looking for.
Met through
My Job Glasses.
What I learned from her
My conversation with Anahid Hanimyan helped me understand how to benefit from artificial intelligence in my learning and future work. She highlighted the importance of understanding different types of AI, including generative AI, and knowing which tools are suitable for different tasks. She also clarified the differences between data analysts and data scientists. Data analysts often focus on analysing existing data, creating reports and dashboards, and supporting business decisions. Data scientists often develop statistical and machine-learning models to identify patterns and make predictions. This exchange gave me a clearer picture of careers in data and the skills associated with each role.
